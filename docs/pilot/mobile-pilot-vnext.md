# Mobile pilot — vNext shell (Guide · Map · Today · Plan · Settings)

A 20–30 minute test of the five-tab structure on a real phone. You are
testing where things LIVE and how the screens read, not how pretty they are —
say out loud where you expect something before you look for it.

What changed since the first shell: **Today sits in the centre** of the
bottom bar (slightly raised — it is still a tab, not a button), Today no
longer has a Prepare mode (preparation lives under **Plan**), **Guide** is a
four-tile dossier (Stages & highlights, Stops & places, Shops & supplies,
Transport), **Plan** is a dashboard (Day plan and Packing on top, Travel &
stays and Wallet below — Travel and Wallet are separate now), and Guide and
Plan carry their own topographic themes (blue and copper — real Kungsleden
contours, like Today's green).

## Install

- **Live URL:** https://algolon.github.io/Fjallkompis/
- **Android (Chrome):** open the URL → the app offers "Install Fjällkompis",
  or use ⋮ → *Add to Home screen* → *Install*. Open it from the home screen
  afterwards so it runs as a standalone app (no address bar).
- **Update/refresh:** reopen the app while online; when a toast offers an
  update, accept it. (No toast = you are already current.)
- **Offline assets:** Settings → *Offline maps* → download the basemap
  (~6 MB; terrain and satellite are optional and larger). Settings → *Trail
  readiness* shows what is stored.
- **Reset personal test data:** Settings → *Backup & restore* → *Reset local
  data*. This clears plan, packing, travel items and stored documents — the
  app itself stays installed, so you can start over without reinstalling.

## Test scenario (20–30 min)

1. **Orientation (no instructions):** open the app and look around for two
   minutes. Say what you think each of the five tabs is for, and what the
   raised centre item does.
2. **Prepare a trip:** open Plan. Create a Day plan, mark a few packing
   items and read the Packing card's numbers back on the Plan home — do
   they match what you did?
3. **Find trail information:** how long is stage 3, does Sälka have a shop,
   and find one viewpoint or side trip. (Expected home: Guide.)
4. **Save a transport item:** find the bus from Nikkaluokta (Guide →
   Transport) and add it to your trip. Check where it ended up.
5. **Store a document:** add any PDF or photo to the Wallet (Plan →
   Wallet). Then check Travel & stays — is it clear why the bus lives there
   and the document lives in the Wallet?
6. **Open the route on the Map:** from Today, use *View route*; from a
   stage, use *View on map*.
7. **Reopen offline:** enable flight mode, close the app fully, reopen it.
   Navigate through all five tabs, including a Guide and a Plan sub-page.
8. **Tonight & membership:** on Today, open Tonight's stop and the STF
   membership card (add one under Plan → Wallet first if you have one).

## Feedback questions

1. Did the centred Today feel like the home screen or like an action button?
2. Was its raised treatment useful or too dominant?
3. Could you distinguish Guide from Plan without explanation?
4. Did *Stages & highlights* match what you expected to find behind it?
5. Did the Day plan card feel like the primary planning workspace?
6. Did the Packing card tell you anything useful before opening the list?
7. Were Needed, Ready and Packed understandable? And pack weight vs worn
   weight?
8. Did Travel & stays and Wallet feel meaningfully different?
9. Were the empty-state instructions helpful when a section was still empty?
10. Did the blue (Guide) and copper (Plan) backgrounds help you know where
    you were — and did everything stay readable outdoors, in daylight?

Report issues with Settings → *Copy diagnostic summary* (technical facts
only — versions, platform, offline asset status; never personal data) pasted
into your message.
