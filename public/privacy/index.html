Fjallkompis

Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 August 2026

Fjallkompis does not collect your personal data, and it has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

Everything you enter — your trip plan, packing list, notes and the documents you keep in the Trail Wallet — is stored only on the device you entered it on. It is not sent to us, and there is no server that holds a copy.

What Fjallkompis is

Fjallkompis is an offline hiking companion for the northern Kungsleden in Swedish Lapland. It brings route, stage, hut, transport and packing information together in one place, alongside your own navigation tools. It is published by Algolon and is available as a web app (a “PWA”) and as an Android app. Both are the same application, and this policy covers both.

Data you create stays on your device

Fjallkompis stores what you enter in your browser’s or the app’s own local storage on your device:

  • your route direction, day plan, trip items, packing list, journal entries, hut notes and app settings;
  • the documents you add to the Trail Wallet — for example a scanned passport, an insurance certificate or an STF membership card — including their files;
  • any offline map data you choose to download, which is stored as map files, not as anything about you.

None of this leaves your device unless you deliberately export it yourself. Fjallkompis has no user accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync and no backend that stores your data.

On Android, Fjallkompis also switches off Android’s automatic backup and device-to-device transfer, so your trip data and Trail Wallet documents are not copied to Google Drive or to a new phone behind your back.

Location

Fjallkompis can use your device’s location for two features you start yourself: showing where you are on the map, and following your progress along the trail while you walk.

  • Your device asks for your permission first, and you can refuse or withdraw it at any time in your browser or Android settings. The app remains fully usable without location.
  • Location is used only while the app is open and in front of you. There is no background tracking, and the Android app deliberately does not request background-location permission.
  • Your position is used as it arrives and is held only in the app’s memory while the screen is open. It is not saved to your device’s storage, not included in exports or backups, and never sent anywhere. Closing or reloading the app discards it.

Network connections

Fjallkompis is built to work offline. There are only two reasons it touches the network at all, and both are requests for its own files from its own address:

  • loading the app itself — the same page, script and image files any website loads, after which the app keeps working without a connection;
  • downloading offline map data, when you choose to in Settings.

Separately from both, tapping a link to another organisation’s website takes you there in your browser — see “Links to other websites” below.

Those files are served by GitHub, which hosts the app on GitHub Pages. The optional map downloads — terrain relief and satellite imagery — are the same published files in both places: the web app fetches them from the app’s own address, and the Android app fetches them from the project’s file releases on GitHub, so that an installed version always receives the exact map data it was built for. As with any website, GitHub’s servers can see the ordinary technical details of a request — your IP address, the file requested, the time and your browser or device type. That is standard hosting infrastructure, it happens because you loaded a page, and Fjallkompis neither adds anything to it nor receives it. GitHub’s handling of that data is covered by GitHub’s privacy statement. The Android app is installed from Google Play, and Google Play’s own handling of installs and updates is covered by Google’s privacy policy.

Fjallkompis sends no personal data, no identifiers, no usage data, no location and none of your content to us or to anyone else. There is no server we operate that receives anything from the app.

Links to other websites

Some screens link out to other organisations — mountain stations, bus and train operators, map data providers. Opening one of those links leaves Fjallkompis and opens that site in your browser, where that organisation’s own privacy policy applies. Fjallkompis passes no information about you along with the link.

No analytics, tracking or advertising

Fjallkompis contains no analytics, no usage measurement, no crash or error reporting service, no advertising, no cookies used for tracking, and no third-party software development kits that collect data.

Settings has a “Copy diagnostic summary” button for reporting problems. It copies a short, fixed list of technical facts — app version, platform, whether offline map data is present — to your clipboard so that you can paste it into a message if you choose to. It contains none of your personal data and is not sent anywhere by the app.

Backup and export

Settings lets you export your data yourself: a complete backup file (which includes your Trail Wallet documents) or a lighter data export (which does not). You choose where the file is saved, using your device’s own file picker. From that point the file is yours to look after — a complete backup contains your personal documents, so keep it somewhere private. Importing a file replaces the data currently on the device.

Deleting your data

  • Reset local data in Settings clears your trip data and stored Wallet documents on that device.
  • Removing an offline map in Settings deletes those map files from the device.
  • Uninstalling the Android app, or clearing the site data for the web app in your browser, removes everything Fjallkompis has stored on that device.

Because nothing is stored anywhere else, there is no account to close and no copy for us to delete — deleting it on your device is the whole of it. Any backup file you exported yourself is not affected; delete that file if you no longer want it.

Children

Fjallkompis is a hiking reference tool intended for a general audience. It is not directed at children, and it collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the way Fjallkompis handles data changes, this page is updated before or alongside the release that changes it, and the “last updated” date at the top changes with it. This page is always the current version.
